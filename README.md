# 🍕 Pizza Sales Data Analysis

## 📊 Project Overview

This project analyzes pizza sales data to understand **sales performance, customer ordering patterns, pizza category and size preferences, and best- and worst-selling products**.

The dashboard provides a visual overview of key business metrics and highlights patterns that can help a pizza business understand its sales performance and customer demand.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

* Analyze overall pizza sales performance
* Track important sales KPIs
* Identify the busiest days and ordering hours
* Understand sales contribution by pizza category and size
* Identify the top-performing and lowest-performing pizzas
* Generate meaningful business insights from the sales data

## 🔍 Key Insights

### 🗓️ Orders by Day & Time

* Orders are highest on **weekends, particularly Friday and Saturday evenings**.
* The highest order activity occurs around **12–1 PM and 4–8 PM**.

### 🍕 Sales by Category & Size

* The **Classic** pizza category contributes the maximum sales and total orders.
* **Large-size pizzas** contribute the highest share of sales.

### ⭐ Best & Worst Sellers

* **Chicken pizzas** are among the top revenue generators.
* **The Brie Carre Pizza** is at the bottom in both orders and revenue.

### 📊 Product Performance

The dashboard compares the **Top 5 Best Sellers** and **Bottom 5 Worst Sellers** based on total pizzas sold, helping identify products with strong and weak customer demand.
